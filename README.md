Entry for the [Texts From Your Future Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e) bounty: one funny text message, under 200 characters, sent back from the writer's future self.

The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> future you here. that "temp" username "xX_DarkSoul_69_Xx" you made for one Elden Ring run? it's now the verified handle for the Vatican's official exorcism livestream. pope follows. bless.

## Why this one

The joke is recognition, not randomness: "DarkSoul" already sounds like exorcism jargon, so a throwaway gamer tag becoming the Vatican's verified exorcism handle clicks instead of merely surprising. The exact handle, the single Elden Ring run, and the "pope follows. bless." button make it one specific person's future — and the button lands twice, as an absurd detail and as a gamer sign-off.
